      Trading Risk Disclosure

      ARIA REAL-1 is a paper-trading product. It observes real, live Solana market data (Pump.fun, PumpSwap, Raydium, Meteora) and runs real safety/eligibility evaluation against it, but every buy and sell it produces is simulated. No transaction is ever built, signed, or broadcast to Solana, and no funds — yours or ARIA's — are ever at risk from ARIA's trading activity in this beta. There is no "live" mode enabled in this release; the "REAL-2" tier shown above is explicitly disabled and unavailable.

      Paper results are a simulation, not a guarantee. Simulated fees, slippage, and fills are modeled to be realistic but will differ from what a real execution would experience — a strong paper result does not guarantee a real-money result would match it, if and when a live execution capability is ever released. Cryptocurrency markets, including the tokens ARIA observes, are extremely volatile and carry a high risk of loss for anyone who trades them with real funds, independent of anything ARIA does.

      ARIA is software, not financial, investment, or trading advice. Nothing in this product is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.
